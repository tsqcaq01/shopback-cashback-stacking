## How ShopBack Cashback Works — The Three Rules That Explain Everything

A lot of confusion around cashback platforms dissolves once you understand the economics underneath. Here's the whole model in three sentences:

1. **Merchants pay commission to anyone who refers a paying customer.** That budget funds creators, comparison sites, deal blogs, and cashback apps alike.
2. **ShopBack registers as one of those referrers** and passes the bulk of the commission back to you as real cash.
3. **The referral has to be tracked.** No click-through from ShopBack, no attribution, no commission, no cashback.

Everything else — pending vs confirmed timing, category exclusions, why Amazon pays 1.5% but Temu pays 50% during a campaign, why a coupon code from a third-party site can break your cashback — flows from those three rules.

3. **Pay with that linked card at the till.** Tap, swipe, or insert all work.

The reason this works invisibly is that in-store cashback runs on the **payment network**, not on browser cookies. It also doesn't conflict with your card's own rewards. Chase Ultimate Rewards, Citi ThankYou points, AMEX Membership Rewards, Capital One Miles — those are paid by the card issuer. In-store cashback is paid by the merchant. Different rails, both apply.

## Tips to Keep Cashback Tracking Reliably

Cashback platforms have a small set of failure modes. Avoid them and the platform basically runs itself.

- **Always start your session at ShopBack.** The click-through creates the referral, earns ShopBack its commission, and unlocks your share. Skip this step and there's no cashback to track, period.
- **Disable ad-blockers and cashback-blockers** on the merchant's checkout page. They can strip the tracking cookie.
- **Clear competing affiliate cookies** before starting, or use a fresh browser session. If another affiliate site set a cookie earlier in the day, the merchant may attribute the sale to them instead of ShopBack.
- **Avoid coupon-site detours** between clicking through from ShopBack and completing checkout. Third-party codes can override the affiliate attribution.
- **Stick to promo codes listed on ShopBack.** Those are merchant-approved and cashback-compatible.
- **For purchases you might return**, wait for the return-window decision before counting the cashback as yours. Refunds reverse cashback.
- **If a purchase doesn't track**, file a missing-cashback claim in the ShopBack app at *Account → Cashback → Missing Cashback* within the claim window stated on the merchant's page.
